# Erlang and Elixir — Laboratory Solutions 2025
Solutions for the Erlang and Elixir course at AGH University of Science and Technology (academic year 2024–2025). The course focuses on functional and concurrent programming, process management and OTP.

## Laboratories

| Laboratory | Topic                                      | Link          |
| ---------- | ------------------------------------------------ | --------------- |

| Lab 2      | Introduction to Erlang – Part II                 | [lab2](./lab2/) |
| Lab 3      | Processes and Messaging in Erlang                | [lab3](./lab3/) |
| Lab 4      | OTP & rebar – GenServer, Supervisor              | [lab4](./lab4/) |
| Lab 5      | Introduction to Elixir & integration with Erlang | [lab5](./lab5/) |
| Lab 6      | Databases in Elixir: mix & Ecto                  | [lab6](./lab6/) |
| Lab 7      | Web application in Phoenix                       | [lab7](./lab7/) |

## Prerequisites

* Erlang/OTP 26 or higher
* Elixir 1.16 or higher
* rebar3 (for Erlang projects)
* mix (for Elixir projects)
